🌎 Theoretical & Practical Geodesy in Multi-Hemisphere Atoll Environments

The Republic of Kiribati encompasses 33 islands dispersed across 3.44 million km² of ocean, straddling both the Equator and the 180° meridian. This creates "The Three-Zone Problem"—spatial fragmentation across the Northern and Southern Hemispheres, Eastern/Western Longitudes, and non-contiguous UTM zones. The MFOR-GSD Spatial Reference Rule enforces master unprojected storage in WGS 84 (EPSG:4326/4327) combined with on-demand projection into native UTM zones during metric analysis.

✗ STRICTLY PROHIBITED Unadjusted EGM2008 Conversions

The 400mm EGM2008 Gravimetric Tilt Artifact

Global geopotential models (EGM2008) exhibit an unmodeled ~400 mm (0.40 m) gravimetric tilt across Tarawa. Because natural terrain elevation rarely exceeds 3.0 m, applying EGM2008 introduces a 13.3% vertical full-scale error, artificially submerging whole villages on one end while elevating vulnerable causeways on the other.

Physical Cause: Steep basaltic volcanic seamounts (ρ ≈ 2.9 g/cm³) rising 4,000m from abyssal ocean water (ρ ≈ 1.03 g/cm³) create extreme gravity gradients truncated at degree/order 2160 in satellite altimetry.

📏 National Vertical Reference Frame (Betio TGZ) & Outer-Island TBM SOP

All coastal topographic and bathymetric elevations are standardized to Betio Tide Gauge Zero (TGZ), anchored by Primary Benchmark KIR 1 (+3.5334 m TGZ). Direct ellipsoidal-to-orthometric raster conversions utilize a single scalar vertical translation parameter ΔZ = ZKIR1, TGZ - zKIR1, Ellipsoid to preserve raw RTK/PPK surface slope free of global geoid tilt.

🚨 MANDATORY PROTOCOL: D-RTK 2 Base Station Setup

Autonomous single-point base station averaging is STRICTLY PROHIBITED. In equatorial ionospheric environments, single-point averaging introduces 1.50m to 3.00m vertical shifts. Field operators MUST execute manual coordinate entry in DJI Pilot 2 before arming aircraft motors:

1. Plumb over benchmark KIR 1 or certified MFOR-TBM.
2. Verify locking tripod APC height offset = 1.8015 m.
3. Enter certified benchmark lat/long to 9 decimal places.

💻 Data Processing Pipeline, Hydro-Conditioning & ASPRS Accuracy Certification

Raw LiDAR point clouds undergo strip alignment in DJI Terra Pro, bare-earth extraction via the Cloth Simulation Filter (CSF), depression breaching for culverts, and marine hydro-flattening. Accuracy certification is governed by ASPRS 2023 Edition 2 Standards using independent Checkpoints.

🧵 Cloth Simulation Filter (CSF) Mandatory Parameters

The 3D point cloud is inverted upside down; a simulated rigid cloth is dropped under gravity to filter dense coconut palms and mangrove roots while preserving carbonate terrain micro-relief.

cloth_resolution (Grid Resolution): 1.0 m
rigidness (Cloth Stiffness): 1 (Steep Slope / Scarp)
classification_threshold (h_cc): 0.15 m
iterations / time_step: 500 / 0.65

💧 Depression Breaching vs. Taro Pit Preservation

⚠️ PROHIBITION: Standard "Fill Depressions" Banned

Global depression filling erases authentic babai (giant swamp taro) pits and borrow pits, destroying real flood storage capacity. Analysts must use Least-Cost Depression Breaching in WhiteboxTools (max_dist = 25.0m) to cut only artificial causeway road blockages.

Marine Hydro-Flattening:

Water-edge breakline polygons are digitized at low tide. Oceanward and lagoonward raster cells seaward of the breakline are burned to instantaneous water height (+0.500m TGZ) or NoData to guarantee flat hydrodynamic boundary conditions.

🛡 Spatial Data Governance, OGC Standards & Four-Tier Commercial Licensing

MFOR-GSD mandates the formal deprecation of ESRI Shapefiles in favor of OGC GeoPackage (.gpkg) and Cloud Optimized GeoTIFF (.tif/COG). All products are governed by CARE Indigenous Data Sovereignty principles and metadata harvested via ISO 19115-1 XML to the Kiribati National Repository and SPC Pacific Data Hub.

Format Mandate: Legacy Shapefile Deprecation .SHP PROHIBITED

✗ Legacy Shapefile Vulnerabilities (.shp)
  • 10-character attribute header truncation mangles field names (crest_elev_tgz_m → crest_el_1).
  • Multi-file sidecar fragmentation causes lost projection (.prj) files during island transfers.
  • Strict 2 GB file size ceiling prevents storing atoll-wide building or shoreline vector tables.
✓ Mandated OGC GeoPackage Standard (.gpkg)
  • Single, self-contained SQLite3 database container supporting up to 140 TB.
  • Full UTF-8 character encoding preserves native Gilbertese place names.
  • Embedded spatial indexing (R*Tree), vector layer styles, and ISO metadata tables.
